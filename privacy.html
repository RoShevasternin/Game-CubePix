Privacy Policy

CubePix — the Android game, its web demo and this website · effective 2026-09-30

The short version
  • CubePix has no accounts. We never ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • The Android app uses Google services for ads, purchases, analytics, crash reports, leaderboards and holiday events. Those services process some device data, as described below. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent before ads are personalised.
  • This website and the web demo use no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no third-party services. Your demo progress stays in your browser.

Who we are

CubePix is made by Lewydo, a two-person indie game studio from Ukraine (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the data described here. Contact: roshevasternin@gmail.com.

This policy covers the CubePix app for Android (the “App”), the free web demo at https://roshevasternin.github.io/Game-CubePix/play/ (the “Demo”) and this website (the “Site”).

The Android app

Progress and settings — on your device

Your pictures, stars, coins, keys, settings and similar game state are stored on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, they may be included in your device backup under your Google account settings. We do not receive this data.

Ads and your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform appears before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the App’s settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded videos are always optional: nothing in the game requires watching one.
  • If you buy “No ads”, banners and full-screen ads between levels and at launch stop.

Why we process data (legal bases under the GDPR)

  • Consent: personalised ads, where the law requires consent.
  • Contract: delivering the in-app purchases you make.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalised ads that keep the game free, analytics and crash reports that help us improve the game, fraud prevention and security.

The web demo and this website

  • They use no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. The fonts are served from this site itself, not from a third party.
  • The Demo saves your progress and settings, and the Site remembers the language you picked, in your browser’s local storage on your device. Nothing of it is sent to us. To remove it, clear this site’s data in your browser.
  • The Site and the Demo are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Links to Google Play and to our studio’s website are ordinary links. The pages they open have their own privacy policies.

Children

CubePix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the higher age of digital consent that applies in your country. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your device stays until you delete the App, clear its data, or clear this site’s data in your browser.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept for a limited time (at most 14 months) and then deleted or anonymised.
  • Data held by Google services is kept under Google’s retention policies and your Google account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. This includes the rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA in California. We do not sell your personal information. To use any of these rights, write to roshevasternin@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your data

  • Game progress: uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings. In the Demo, clear this site’s data in your browser.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • Leaderboard scores: manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings.
  • Anything else: write to us, and we will delete what we can link to you or help with a request to Google.

International transfers and security

Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States. For transfers from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it relies on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. We use reputable providers and keep what we process to what the game needs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. For important changes, we will also tell you in the App.
